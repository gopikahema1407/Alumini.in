PLATFORM OVERVIEW
================================================================================
AluminiX is a complete AI-powered alumni networking platform for KIT connecting 
students with alumni mentors, featuring social networking, job opportunities, 
and AI-powered career guidance.

Status: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL & PRODUCTION READY
Version: 2.0 (With AI, Social Features & Admin)
Launch Date: August 23, 2026

- Framework: Flask
- Server: Python WSGI
- Port: 5000 (default)

- HTML5 (Semantic markup)

- Features: RLS, real-time, connection pooling

- Models: Llama 3 (8B), Mistral 7B
